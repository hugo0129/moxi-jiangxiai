## 🔭 My Perspective on the "Jiangxi AI Circle"

### 1. What It Is vs. What It Is Not
- **NOT an amphitheater for speculative hype**: We do not engage in empty grand narratives, chase fleeting tech jargon, or run predatory training/middleman schemes;
- **IS a pragmatic, grass-roots "Community of Practice"**: Spontaneously formed by local software developers, business founders, young creators, and super-individuals. Members bring authentic business bottlenecks, real-world workflows, and genuine data constraints to exchange the most practical code solutions and efficiency gains.

### 2. Development Trajectory: From Grassroots to National Stages
- **Local Roots, Multi-City Resonance**:
  - **August 2025**: Initiated in Nanchang; held the 1st offline salon in October, igniting local peer connection;
  - **December 2025**: Tengwang Pavilion New Year Gathering, establishing the core consensus of **"Real Business Implementation"**;
  - **Provincial Spark**: Jiujiang (Dec 2025), Ganzhou (Feb 2026), Yichun (Mar 2026), Fuzhou (Mar 2026), and Jingdezhen (Mar 2026) successively launched their inaugural offline events, breaking the "tech island" isolation across Jiangxi;
  - **Competitions & Night Schools**: Hosted Jiangxi's first OpenClaw × OPC Crawfish Jam (supported by MiniMax), GanHackathon #1 (Problem-Driven Hackathon), Nanchang AI Night School, and university technical exchanges, continuously cultivating local talent.
- **Cross-Regional Bridges & National Representation**:
  - Invited to speak at the **1st National AI+OPC Conference in Hangzhou**; highlighted in *Jiangxi Daily* with an in-depth feature titled *"Crossing the Qiantang Tide"*;
  - Spoke on stage at the **2026 AI OPC Entrepreneur Ecosystem Conference in Wenzhou**, co-initiating the *National AI OPC Community Charter* alongside leaders from 10 cities;
  - Invited by the Chengmai Bureau of Science, Industry & IT (Hainan) as Mentor and Pitch Judge for the **"Super-Individual Evolution Camp"**, delivering keynotes and evaluating cutting-edge national projects;
  - Featured at the **Xiamen WOPC BRICS Conference**, sustaining high-frequency exchanges with premier tier-1 tech communities to channel external innovation back into Jiangxi.
- **Deep Industry Fieldwork: Deciphering the OPC Ecosystem**:
  - Conducted on-site research into 5 prominent OPC hubs in Nanchang (**Zhongcan·Maiqi**, **Sibaire**, **Anlan AI**, **XSPACE Yujianli**, **Chunqiu Starlink**), objectively analyzing their operational mechanics and cost models across workflow distribution, short-video pipelines, venture incubation, commercial complexes, and service marketplaces to provide developers with clear, sober decision benchmarks.

### 3. Core Spirit: One Person is a Company (OPC)
The AI era equips individuals with unprecedented leverage. The **"One-Person Company" (OPC)** is not a buzzword, but an entirely new mode of production and organizational structure. The Jiangxi AI Hub adheres to **"Sincere Altruism, Long-term Value, and Pragmatic Implementation"**, ensuring that every developer and creator committed to deep technical craftsmanship can find kindred spirits on the fertile ground of Jiangxi.

## 💻 Local Development & Quick Start

This project is built as a pure, dependency-free static web application. It requires no complex build tools or bundlers and is ready to run out of the box:

```bash

cd compliant-site

# Start a local static HTTP server
python3 -m http.server 8088

# Open in browser
open http://localhost:8088/
```
